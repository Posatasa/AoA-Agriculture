# 1.2.31-beta

The Varsium bucket, and creative stops spending the fluid.

**`aoaagriculture:varsium_bucket`** (超金桶) and its water, lava and candied water variants, from three
varsium ingots, in the gold of varsium blocks. It works like the Elecanium bucket but the other way
round: its **lava** bucket is the one that spends its fluid - used on nothing it comes back empty, with
the pour sound, and the player's **Energy** goes back to full.

**In creative, spending the fluid no longer empties the bucket.** Both buckets stay full and still
refill the resource, which is how a vanilla bucket behaves in creative. That applies to the Elecanium
water bucket as well, so the two now share one class instead of two copies of the same code: the
resource is a parameter (Spirit or Energy) rather than a class each.

The Spirit bridge became a resource bridge for the same reason, and the build checks both resource
fields (`AoAResources.SPIRIT` and `AoAResources.ENERGY`) against the AoA jar. A new check also refuses
a resource bucket that does not look at `instabuild`: that is the creative behaviour above, and it is
the sort of thing that disappears when the spending path is edited. Verified by removing it.

The tab's metal order is now Limonite, Rosite, Emberstone, Blazium, Elecanium, Varsium, Lunar, Shyre,
Dark.
