# 1.3.2-beta

Three chocolate cakes, one per sauce: a vanilla cake with three bowls of sauce poured over it.

Each is vanilla's cake in every respect that can be observed - the block has vanilla cake's own
properties and extends `CakeBlock`, so its states, its bites, its shape, its hardness and the way it is
eaten are all vanilla's. The one thing that cannot be inherited is the amount: vanilla's `eat` is private
and hardcodes two hunger and 0.1 saturation, so `use` is reimplemented with vanilla's own sequence and
only the numbers changed. **A slice gives four hunger and 0.2 saturation, double vanilla's.**

The textures are vanilla's cake texture set tinted proportionally, so the cake keeps its own shading and
the cherries on top take the chocolate with it. White chocolate cake is a clear cream rather than another
white, so it is not mistaken for a plain cake.

Shapeless: three of the matching sauce and a vanilla cake.
