# 1.2.35-beta

Candied water buckets pour again, and the chilli water cauldron has no item of its own.

**The pour was throwing away its own sound.** These buckets hold a fluid that is resolved lazily, which
means vanilla's `content` field is null in them - and vanilla's pour sound reads that field. So the
bucket placed the water and then threw a `NullPointerException` while playing the sound, which left the
action half done and the bucket looking like it had not poured at all. The sound is now taken from the
fluid, and the bucket also never falls back to behaving like an empty bucket: the item is always a
filled one, so if its fluid cannot be resolved it says so once in the log instead.

A failed fluid lookup is also no longer cached, so a lookup that happened too early can no longer leave
every candied water bucket inert for the rest of the session.

**The chilli water cauldron stays a block of its own, but has no item form.** A vanilla cauldron's only
block states are its four levels, so there is no data value left to store "this is chilli water" in -
that is why it is a separate block. What it can do is behave like the vanilla one where it matters:
picking it gives a **vanilla cauldron**, which is also what it was made from and what it turns back
into when the last level is taken. It no longer appears in the bucket tab either, since there is no item
to appear.
