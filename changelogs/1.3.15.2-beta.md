# 1.3.15.2-beta

Two bucket fixes.

**Varsium has a chilli water bucket.** Its family was registered by hand rather than through the shared
helper, and the chilli water kind was missed - so a Varsium bucket could take chilli water out of a
cauldron or a pool and then had nowhere to become. It is registered now, and appears in the bucket tab
with the rest.

**Shyre's chilli water bucket matches the other Shyre buckets.** The chilli buckets were drawn separately
from the others, with one colour per metal, so Shyre's was a flat teal while its empty, water, lava and
candied buckets are all a gem-to-ingot gradient; Elecanium's was flat too while its siblings are half
green and half blue. They are drawn by the same code as every other bucket now, with only the fluid tinted
chilli red, so each metal's chilli bucket is the same metal as its siblings.
