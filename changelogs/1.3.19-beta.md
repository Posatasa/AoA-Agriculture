# 1.3.19-beta

The sashimi icons are the redrawn 32x set.

The new 32x textures already carry their transparent border, so they are installed as they are - no
compression, no extra border. The rainbowfish keeps its redraw and the other twenty-four are the latest
art too.

`tools/install_sashimi_pack.py` installs the pack's top-level 32x textures as-is.
