# 1.2.30-beta

The Elecanium bucket, whose water one buys Spirit.

**`aoaagriculture:elecanium_bucket`** and its water, lava and candied water variants, crafted from
three elecanium ingots in vanilla's bucket shape. Elecanium's ingot is **half green and half blue**,
not one colour, so the icons take both halves separately - left half from the ingot's left half,
right from its right - and the buckets are drawn half green, half blue rather than in a muddy teal
that appears nowhere in AoA's texture.

**The water bucket spends its water on the player.** Used on nothing - right-clicked at the air, where
there is nowhere to pour - the bucket comes back empty, with the pour sound, and the player's Spirit
goes back to full. Over a block it pours water like any other bucket, so the water is what pays for
the Spirit: filling it again means finding water.

Spirit is reached by name at run time, like the Farming experience bridge, and the maximum is read
from the resource rather than assumed, so it fills to whatever AoA says its maximum is. The build now
checks the AoA members that bridge needs *and* the names the bridge spells out, so a typo there fails
the build instead of silently disabling the effect.

The bucket sits after Blazium in the creative tab's metal order: Limonite, Rosite, Emberstone,
Blazium, Elecanium, Lunar, Shyre, Dark.
