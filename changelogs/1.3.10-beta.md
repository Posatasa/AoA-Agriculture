# 1.3.10-beta

The cakes and their slices use hand-drawn art.

Everything the seven cakes and the eight slices draw now comes from a supplied asset pack: 43 textures,
already 16 by 16 - four faces and an inventory icon for each cake, one icon for each slice. They replace
the textures that were being generated, so nothing is resampled or tinted any more, and the pack is the
source: `tools/art` holds it file for file, and the generator copies it in after its cleaning pass.

The models, blockstates, recipes and names are unchanged by any of this - the cakes are still vanilla's
cake block with a doubled serving, the honey cakes still feed what their base cake feeds, and a cake still
cuts into seven slices by hand or on a Farmer's Delight cutting board.
