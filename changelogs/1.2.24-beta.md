# 1.2.24-beta

Bottling candied water no longer uses up the water.

Candied water is an endless pool - the Shyre water bucket has treated it that way since 1.2.14 - so a
glass bottle should cost nothing, and the source block should still be there afterwards. It is: the
fill now only checks that the block is a full candied water source and leaves it alone, instead of
taking the fluid the way a vanilla bucket would.

The check is the same one that taking it would have made: a full block, not a flowing edge. Nothing
else changes - the bottle, its sound, the statistics and the cancelled vanilla click all stay.

The build now refuses a `Bottles` that takes fluid out of the world, with the reason in the message,
because that single call is what consumed the source. Verified by putting the call back and watching
the build fail.
