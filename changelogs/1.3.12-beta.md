# 1.3.12-beta

Three things to make from chilli water.

**Chilli paste** - a bowl, a bottle of chilli water and a sugar, shapeless. It feeds nothing at all, like
the water it is made from, and it sets the eater alight for a second unless they are fire resistant or
fire immune; the bottle comes back from the craft and the bowl from the meal.

**Spicy steak** - a bowl of the paste over a cooked steak, bowl returned. Ten hunger and 0.8 saturation,
and it does not burn: the chilli is cooked into the meat.

**Chilli paste bread** - two loaves and a bowl of the paste, bowl returned. Eight hunger and 0.8
saturation, and no burn either.

The icons are from a supplied pack, three 16 by 16 textures installed from it the same way as the cakes.
The paste carries a tooltip, since "feed nothing, set yourself on fire" is worth a warning.
