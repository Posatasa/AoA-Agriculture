# 1.3.6-beta

Honey cakes, and cake slices for all eight cakes.

**Honey cakes.** A bottle of honey poured over a cake makes the honey cake for that cake: vanilla's, and
one for each of the three chocolate cakes. The bottle comes back, the way a milk bucket does in a cake,
because vanilla's honey bottle already has a glass bottle as its crafting remainder. The textures are the
base cake's blended towards honey, so a honey chocolate cake still reads as its cake with honey on it. A
honey cake feeds exactly what its base cake feeds a slice, since honey is a topping rather than a second
helping.

**Cake slices.** Every cake now has one - vanilla's, the three chocolate ones and the four honey ones -
and a slice is the serving, eaten from the hand: two hunger and 0.1 saturation for the vanilla and honey
cakes, four and 0.2 for the chocolate ones, matching the cake it came from. Seven per cake, which is what
a cake is: the whole cake plus its six bites.

* by hand: one cake in the crafting grid gives seven slices;
* with Farmer's Delight: the same on a cutting board, with any knife, in `data/farmersdelight/`, so it
  does not exist without Farmer's Delight.

**The slice icons** are drawn rather than photographed: each is a wedge in its cake's own icing and body
colours, sampled from that cake's icon - the icing from the brighter quarter and the body from the middle
band, so a plain cake slice comes out cream rather than the red of its jam band. There is no pixel-art
skill available here, and a wedge built from the cake's own palette keeps the set consistent.
