# 1.2.41-beta

Chilli water is translucent now - the texture was never the problem.

Fluids are drawn through their *block's* render layer, and vanilla registers water and lava there by
hand, water as translucent. A fluid block of our own is registered nowhere, so it got the default solid
layer, which draws it opaque no matter how transparent its texture is. AoA never hit this because
candied water reuses vanilla's water textures and so rides on vanilla's registration.

Our block is registered as translucent in client setup now. The texture work of the last two releases
still matters - it is what gives the fluid water's shading and its see-through alpha - but this was the
reason none of it showed.
