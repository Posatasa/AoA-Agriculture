# 1.2.32-beta

Chilli water: a fluid of this mod's own, made with AoA's chillies.

**`aoaagriculture:chilli_water`** - a water-like fluid, in the same red as its name suggests. It flows
with water's own numbers and is in the `minecraft:water` tag, so it slows movement and can be swum in
like candied water. It has no item of its own, exactly like candied water: buckets and bottles hold it.

**It is made in a cauldron.** Thirty-two AoA chillies (`aoa3:chilli`) used on a cauldron of water turn
it into a cauldron of chilli water, one cauldron being a bucket, which is three bottles. A player
carrying fewer than thirty-two is told so in chat instead of having nothing happen; creative uses
nothing up and does not demand the count.

**Taking it out:** a vanilla bucket gives the plain chilli water bucket, and every bucket of this mod
gives its own - all except Emberstone, Blazium and Dark, which only take lava and refuse it. A glass
bottle takes one level, a third of the cauldron. Emptying either gives the vanilla bucket or bottle
back, and emptying the cauldron gives the vanilla cauldron back.

**Drinking it** feeds nothing at all and sets the player on fire for a second.

**Standing in it does not put fires out, and it sets entities alight.** This is the one place it cannot
simply be water, so an entity mixin skips vanilla's extinguish and applies the burn instead. Fire
Resistance and Frost Walker both protect from it, as asked.

The textures are vanilla's own water textures tinted to the chilli colour, animation frames and all,
and the cauldron's models are vanilla's cauldron models with the water swapped for chilli water.
