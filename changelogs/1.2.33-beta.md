# 1.2.33-beta

The cauldron, for the buckets that should work with it - and one recipe.

**Water buckets work with cauldrons now.** Vanilla's cauldron names `minecraft:water_bucket` by
identity when it is filled or emptied, so a water bucket from this mod was not part of either
interaction: pouring into a cauldron did nothing, and taking water out of a full one did nothing. Both
are handled by the buckets themselves now - a water bucket fills a cauldron, a full cauldron fills the
right family's water bucket, and the empty buckets of families without a water bucket refuse it, as
they always did. Lava buckets leave a cauldron alone, because a cauldron takes water and nothing else.

Candied water and chilli water buckets stay unable to touch a cauldron, as asked.

**Chilli water is bottled only from its cauldron.** A glass bottle on the fluid now does nothing at
all: it used to be stopped from making a *water* bottle, which is right, but it also handed out a
chilli water bottle, which is not what was asked. The fluid is in the water tag - for swimming and for
slowing movement - so the click has to be stopped deliberately rather than left to vanilla.

**Hellfire from a chilli water bottle**: a shapeless recipe of one chilli water bottle, one gunpowder
and one limonite nugget makes AoA's own `aoa3:hellfire`.

Also: chilli water's resources are generated by the main generator now, through
`tools/generate_chilli_water.py`, instead of a script that had to be run separately and in the right
order.
