# 1.3.18-beta

Sashimi for every AoA fish that can be cut.

Twenty-five fish, from the rainbowfish to the yellow gemtrap, each cut into four slices of itself. A slice
is two hunger and 0.5 saturation, eaten raw - none of them cooks into anything yet - and one fish is cut
into four, either by hand in the crafting grid or on Farmer's Delight's cutting board with any knife.

The textures are the supplied 32 by 32 set, kept at that size: Minecraft scales them onto the item quad,
so the finer art survives. `raw_rainbowfish` is the one fish whose item name differs from the slice's, and
the recipes point at the right thing.
