# 1.2.27-beta

The Dark bucket is actually black now.

Its colour came from darkly powder, which is black - but it was then drawn at the brightness the
bucket icons are laid out at, which turned (66, 66, 66) into a light grey. Darkly powder's own
brightness is the point of taking its colour, so the bucket is now drawn at roughly a third of that
white-reference brightness: a mean luminance of 24 against blazium's 66 and limonite's 116, with the
shading still visible on the rim and the body.
