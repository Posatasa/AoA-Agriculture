# 1.3.3-beta

The cakes have proper inventory icons, and the colours are chocolate rather than tan.

They were showing the block model in the slot, so a cake in the inventory was a little 3D cake. They use
vanilla's cake icon now, tinted, the way vanilla's own cake does.

The tint was also too pale: it scaled by brightness against the icon's average, and since a cake icon is
mostly near-white that pushed the bright pixels *past* the colour instead of to it. The scale is clamped
at one now, so the brightest pixels land exactly on the colour and the milk and dark ones finally read as
milk and dark chocolate. The block textures are made the same way, so they match.
