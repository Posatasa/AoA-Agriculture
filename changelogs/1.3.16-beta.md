# 1.3.16-beta

Six more bucket families, one per AoA ingot that was left out, and the steak and porkchop icons swapped
back the way round they belong.

Every one of them has the ordinary set - empty, water, lava, candied water and chilli water - and the
same bucket recipes as the rest. Lyon's is drawn in two colours because its ingot is: grey down one side
and gold down the other, the way Elecanium's is green and blue.

| metal | what it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Skeletal** (骷髅锭) | nothing. Four ordinary buckets, which is the point of it. |
| **Baronyte** (爵士锭) | its lava bucket spends the lava to refill **Rage**, as Varsium's does Energy. |
| **Ghastly** (阴森锭) | its water bucket saved on the way down: right-click the air and it pours the water onto the first ground below you, and empties. |
| **Ghoulish** (恐惧锭) | its empty bucket fills from water it can *see* - thirty blocks of line of sight - so water out of reach can still be taken. |
| **Lyon** (焦铁锭) | one fill in five also drops the contents of AoA's crystal box loot table at your feet. |
| **Mystite** (神秘锭) | used with no night vision it gives five minutes of it, and keeps the water. With night vision already running it is an ordinary water bucket. |

**Rusted iron is not in the set.** It is an ingot in name only - the others are the metals AoA's own
buckets would be made of - and there is no rusted iron tier to build a bucket from.

Notes on the two judgement calls:

* Ghastly's fall save drops the water on the first non-liquid ground beneath the player, so it works while
  falling and also when standing: it is the bucket that empties downward.
* Mystite's night vision does not consume the water, because the request was that it *grants* the effect
  and only uses normally when night vision is already there. Say the word if it should cost the water.
