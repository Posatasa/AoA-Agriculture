# 1.2.34-beta

Water buckets fill cauldrons now, and the creative tab is three tabs.

**The cauldron, properly this time.** A cauldron is not an `ILiquidContainer` - it carries its own level
and its own `setWaterLevel`, which is why vanilla's bucket special-cases it by item identity rather than
by interface. The pour path was written against the interface, so it never recognised a cauldron and
the bucket fell through to vanilla, which does not know it either. It now uses the cauldron's own
`setWaterLevel`, for water only: a lava bucket still leaves a cauldron alone, and candied water and
chilli water buckets still cannot touch one.

**Three creative tabs**, as asked:

* **AoA Agriculture: Farmland** - the 22 farmland blocks, plain and fertilised, in soil order;
* **AoA Agriculture: Buckets** - every empty bucket and every variant of one, metal by metal in the
  usual order, then the chilli water cauldron;
* **AoA Agriculture: Food** - the candied water bottle and the chilli water bottle.

They live in a new `registry/Tabs` class rather than one per registry: an item names its tab while it
is registering, and a tab names an item for its icon, so in one class that would be a circular static
initialiser. The build now checks that all three tabs exist, that each class still supplies the filler
its tab calls, and that the two item tabs finish with the catch-all - so an item added later cannot go
missing from its tab.
