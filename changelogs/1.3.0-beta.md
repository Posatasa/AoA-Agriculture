# 1.3.0-beta

Three chocolate sauces, one per chocolate block AoA adds.

Each is a bowl of sauce - four hunger, saturation 0.8, a stack of one, and the bowl comes back - made
from three of the matching block and a wooden bowl:

`
   
CCC
 B 
`

With Farmer's Delight installed there is also a cooking pot recipe for each: two blocks instead of
three, taken out with a bowl, eight seconds in the pot. Those live under data/farmersdelight/, so
they simply do not exist when Farmer's Delight does not.

The icons are vanilla's mushroom stew recoloured: the soup takes the chocolate's colour and the bowl
keeps its own pixels, so they read as stew in a bowl rather than as a brown blob.
