# 1.2.42-beta

Chilli water is drawn through the fluid's own render layer now.

Forge gives fluids their own RenderTypeLookup.setRenderLayer(Fluid, RenderType), and that - not the
block's layer - is what a fluid is drawn with. Vanilla registers water and lava there by hand. AoA's
candied water never needed to because it reuses vanilla's water textures and rides on vanilla's entry;
AoA's own fluids do register there, which is what their setRenderTypes in AoABlocks is for.

Our still and flowing fluids are registered as translucent now, alongside the block.
