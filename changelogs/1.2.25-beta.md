# 1.2.25-beta

The silent bucket, and two more lava buckets.

**The Shyre water bucket has a sound again.** Vanilla's pour plays its sound with the player passed as
the sound's *exception* - correct there, because vanilla pours on both the client and the server and
that player's own client plays it locally. This bucket only pours on the server, so copying that left
the player who poured hearing nothing at all. The sound is now broadcast to everyone, that player
included.

**`aoaagriculture:blazium_bucket`, the Blazium bucket** - three blazium ingots, in the deep red of
blazium blocks. Like the Emberstone bucket it takes lava and nothing else, but its tank holds **eight**
instead of four, and the tooltip counts up to eight.

**`aoaagriculture:dark_bucket`, the Dark bucket** - made at AoA's divine station by using a darkly
upgrade kit on the Blazium lava bucket, which is AoA's own `aoa3:upgrade_kit` recipe type, so the
station does the work. It holds lava endlessly, like the Shyre water bucket holds water: pouring costs
nothing, and holding the button for two seconds empties it into an empty Dark bucket, which fills back
into it from any lava source. Drawn almost black.

Three classes were folded together to make that possible rather than copied: one endless bucket for
both fluids, one lava tank that takes its capacity as a parameter, and one lava-only empty bucket that
finds its own family's filled form. Their tooltips are now looked up by the item's own name, so each
bucket states its own capacity instead of four classes hardcoding one key each.

The build now requires the endless bucket to override the pour sound - that override is the whole fix,
and it is exactly the kind of thing that gets dropped in a refactor. Verified by deleting it and
watching the build fail.
