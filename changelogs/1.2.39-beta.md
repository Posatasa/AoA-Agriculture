# 1.2.39-beta

Chilli water looks like a liquid again, and the language files read in order.

**It was being tinted twice.** The still and flowing textures are already the chilli red, and the fluid
colour multiplied them by a red as well - which is what made the fluid dark and opaque rather than
see-through. The colour is white now, so only the texture colours it, and the texture keeps vanilla
water's own transparency: the block underneath shows through the way it does through water.

**Both language files are written sorted** by the generators, so the keys read in order instead of in
whatever order the passes happened to add them.
