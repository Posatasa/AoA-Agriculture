# 1.2.36-beta

Candied water pours again from this mod's buckets.

The candied and chilli water buckets share one class, but only the chilli one worked - because the
chilli bucket left `use` to vanilla while the candied one used a hand-written copy of it. The copy is
gone: both now pour through vanilla's `use`, and the class only overrides the three things a bucket with
a lazily resolved fluid actually needs to change - the pour itself, its sound, and `getFluid()`.

While there: the Varsium family's candied water bucket was still built the eager way, which resolves its
fluid during item registration - when AoA's fluids do not exist yet. It is lazy now like every other
candied bucket, so it cannot end up holding nothing at all.

And the plain `chilli_water_bucket` - the one a vanilla bucket becomes - now leads the bucket tab, ahead
of the metal buckets.
