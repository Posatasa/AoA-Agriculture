# 1.2.40-beta

Chilli water is tinted the way water is.

The texture was being flattened: every pixel was pushed towards one colour with an offset, which threw
away the shading vanilla water's texture carries and left a solid red slab. It is a proportional tint
now - each pixel keeps its own brightness and is multiplied by the colour - which is what tinting water
looks like, and what candied water looks like beside it. The fluid colour stays white, and the texture
keeps water's own transparency, so the block underneath shows through as it does under water.
